# The errors that ucs-detect 2.3.9 (wcwidth 0.9.4, Unicode 18.0.0) finds in
# the terminal core, one a line: MODE | KEY | ERRORS | reason. MODE is mode
# 2027 (grapheme clustering) on or off. KEY is a category of ucs-detect or
# "language NAME". A key that is not here expects no error.
# tools/conformance/ucs-detect.sh fails when a result has more errors.

# Afterglow joins characters into grapheme clusters as UAX #29 says, and gives
# a cluster the widths that glibc's wcwidth gives its characters, within two
# cells. wcwidth 0.9 goes further in two ways: it counts no width for a virama
# that is a spacing mark (Mc), and it joins a consonant after any virama into
# one conjunct, also where UAX #29 starts a new cluster. Programs that measure
# with glibc expect Afterglow's widths here.
on | language Javanese (Javanese) | 10 | a virama that is a spacing mark (Mc) counts, as in glibc and ghostty
on | language Khün | 71 | a consonant after SAKOT starts a new cluster (UAX #29), as glibc counts
on | language Malayalam | 1 | a consonant after a chillu and virama starts a new cluster (UAX #29), as glibc counts
on | language Sanskrit (Grantha) | 22 | a virama that is a spacing mark (Mc) counts, as in glibc and ghostty
